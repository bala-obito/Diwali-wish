A small Diwali reminder for everyone at home: please be careful when using fireworks.

  • 🧵 Wear cotton clothes and keep a bucket of water nearby.
  • 🎇 Light crackers in an open space, from a safe distance.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Keep little ones and pets safely away, and never hold a lit cracker.
  • 🔥 Never relight a cracker that did not go off. Just soak it in water.
